Carboxylate-Dependent Speciation and Reactivity in Cp*Co(III) Catalysis

This repository contains the literature survey datasets supporting Figure 1C of our manuscript on carboxylate-dependent speciation and reactivity in Cp*Co(III) catalysis.

Literature Surveys

1. Carboxylate Additives in Cp*Co(III) Catalysis

File: Literature cobalt GitHub.xlsx

This dataset summarizes reported Cp*Co(III)-catalyzed C–H functionalization reactions, focusing on the use and identity of carboxylate additives.

2. Reaction Conditions for Weakly Directing Groups (wDGs)

File: wDGs reaction conditions classification_GitHub.xlsx

This dataset summarizes reaction temperatures and times for Cp*Co(III)-catalyzed C–H functionalizations involving weakly directing groups.

Each entry corresponds to a combination of publication, directing group, and reaction conditions.

Temperature classification:

Category 1: <50 °C

Category 2: 50–100 °C

Category 3: ≥100 °C

Reaction time classification:

Category 1: <5 h

Category 2: 5–16 h

Category 3: >16 h

These datasets provide the supporting information for the literature trends presented in Figure 1C.
